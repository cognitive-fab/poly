## Open assumptions in the certificate (owner's reviewer, 2026-10-10)

The certificate states, for each machine with a liveness property, the assumption that no completion of the program's own intents is rejected (S463, PS6.4), as `discharged` or `open`. A channel application is discharged by its generated configuration, and a machine with no rejecting completion handler by its form. **std's `Grant` and `Route` carry it as `open` for v1**, ruled left open: both have a `require` in the handlers of their own completions. My reading of why neither rejection is reachable while its intent is pending (not machine-checked): `Route` leaves `Trying` only through `Answer` or `Fail`, each of which consumes the one pending `call` and emits the next or ends the route, so its `require r == Trying` cannot fail while a `call` is pending. `Grant` is in `Refreshing` from `TokenExpiring` until `Refreshed` or `RefreshFailed`, and the one other action that can take it out is `Revoke`, which ends it in the terminal state, where a late completion is rejected `terminal`, which `PL5409` excepts. This is a reading of the two machines, not a check. A build with a credential or a route therefore shows `open` for those machines, and the gate does not count it as a failure. What would discharge both is the After v1 item "a declared configuration offers the completions of its instances' pending intents".
